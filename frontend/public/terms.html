What the service does

Email Assistant lets you connect Gmail with read-only permission and ask questions about matching emails. Google Gemini generates the answers. The service does not send or delete emails.

Your permission

Connect only an account you own or are authorized to use. You choose whether to grant Gmail access and approve sharing email details with Gemini before each request. Do not use the app to access someone else's account without their permission.

Check the answers

Generated summaries can be incomplete or wrong. Check the original emails before acting, especially for security alerts, deadlines, payments or other important decisions. The app does not provide professional legal, financial or medical advice.
